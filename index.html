How we work

A privately owned holding company

Oya Holdings LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000294221, filed in 2024, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We hold interests for our own account and administer entities within our own group. We are not a broker-dealer, investment adviser or fund manager, we are not registered with the SEC or the Florida Office of Financial Regulation, and we accept no capital from the public. Nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation, and licensed accountants and attorneys are engaged for work that requires a licence.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest in the group?

No. We hold interests for our own account and accept no outside capital. There is no offering here, and nothing on this site is a solicitation.

Do you administer other people's companies?

No. These functions exist for the group's own entities. We are not an accounting firm, a law firm or a registered agent service for third parties.

Why does a holding company need a website?

Mostly so banks, insurers, landlords and counterparties can confirm who we are and find the registered details quickly.

Who does the group's accounting and tax?

Licensed accountants. We keep records in a consistent structure and hand them over; we do not file tax returns or give tax advice ourselves.

Are you registered with the SEC?

No, and we do not need to be. We carry on no securities activity: no funds from others, no advice, no intermediation.

How do I verify the company?

Search document number L24000294221 on the Florida Division of Corporations record at sunbiz.org.

Need to verify the company?

Send an enquiry and we will confirm the registered details and the right contact.
