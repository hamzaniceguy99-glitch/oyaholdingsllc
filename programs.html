What we do, in detail

Four administrative functions, carried out for the group's own entities.

Program 01 · Who owns what, in writing

Ownership & governance records

The question that eventually gets asked by a bank, an insurer or a buyer is simply who owns what, and on what terms. We keep that answerable at any time: current operating agreements, signed resolutions for decisions that need them, and a register that matches reality.

Program 02 · Nothing administratively dissolved

Filings & registrations

Florida administratively dissolves entities that miss an annual report, and it happens to groups that are otherwise well run simply because nobody held the calendar. We hold it: every deadline, every renewal, with reminders well ahead rather than a scramble in September.

Program 03 · Comparable across entities

Bookkeeping coordination

Entities that each keep books their own way cannot be compared or consolidated. We coordinate one chart of accounts, keep intercompany transactions documented rather than implied, and hand licensed accountants records they can actually work from at year end.

Program 04 · Found before they expire

Insurance, leases & contracts

Lapsed coverage is discovered at the worst possible moment, and auto-renewing vendor contracts quietly outlive their usefulness. One register of what is in force, with whom, until when, and what the notice period is, prevents both.
